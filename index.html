§ 05 · The FounderEl Fundador

DerekVinci.

Audio engineer. Label founder. The architect behind the EQS sound since 1998.Ingeniero de audio. Fundador del sello. El arquitecto detrás del sonido EQS desde 1998.

LATIN GRAMMYS VOTING MEMBER
200-SONG PRODUCTION CATALOG
TWENTY-EIGHT YEARS BEHIND THE BOARDVEINTIOCHO AÑOS DETRÁS DE LA CONSOLA
BASED IN ORLANDO, FL

Derek founded EQS Música in 1998 with a specific obsession: bachata, engineered to the same standard as any genre on the global stage. Two decades later, the thesis holds. EQS is the only label positioned exclusively in bachata that plays at an international level — and the artists on the roster are the proof. Derek fundó EQS Música en 1998 con una obsesión específica: la bachata, producida al mismo nivel que cualquier género en el escenario mundial. Dos décadas después, la tesis se mantiene. EQS es el único sello posicionado exclusivamente en bachata que opera a nivel internacional — y los artistas del roster son la prueba.

As a voting member of the Latin Recording Academy and a producer with nearly two hundred songs to his name, Derek operates as both the label's creative director and its technical backbone. Every release passes through his ear before it leaves the building. Como miembro votante de la Academia Latina de Grabación y productor con casi doscientas canciones, Derek opera como director creativo y columna técnica del sello. Cada lanzamiento pasa por su oído antes de salir.
